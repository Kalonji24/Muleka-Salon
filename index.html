About Us

Our Story

Welcome to Muleka Salon, where beauty meets passion. For over 16 years, we have been creating beautiful hairstyles and making women and children feel confident and special.

Our journey started with a simple dream: to help people look and feel their best. What began as a small passion for hairdressing has grown into a full-service salon specializing in African hair care, makeup artistry, and nail services.

At Muleka Salon, we understand African hair. We know how to care for it, style it, and make it shine. From beautiful braids to stunning natural styles, from elegant updos to protective hairstyles - we do it all with love and skill.

16 Years of Experience

With 16 years of experience, we have perfected our craft. Every client who sits in our chair receives expert care and personalized attention. We don't just do hair - we listen to your needs, understand your style, and create looks that make you happy.

Our Promise to You

When you visit Muleka Salon, you're not just a client - you're family. We treat your hair with care, we listen to what you want, and we make sure you leave happy. Every style we create comes from 16 years of experience and a genuine love for making people feel beautiful.

Come visit us in Bellville and experience the Muleka difference. Your hair deserves the best care, and that's exactly what we give.
